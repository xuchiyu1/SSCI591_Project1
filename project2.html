LA County Environmental Burden Explorer

SSCI 591 Project 2 · ArcGIS Experience Builder · CalEnviroScreen 5.0

This web app maps where people in Los Angeles County carry the heaviest combined burden of pollution and social vulnerability. Each of the county's 2,495 census tracts is colored by its overall CalEnviroScreen 5.0 percentile, from light yellow (lower burden) to dark red (higher burden).

Click any tract to see its pollution, health, and poverty indicators. Open the legend to read the color classes, or use the filter to show only the tracts in the 80th percentile or higher.

Data: California Office of Environmental Health Hazard Assessment (OEHHA), CalEnviroScreen 5.0 (2026). Open the app in a new tab.
